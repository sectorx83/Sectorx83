## Executive summary
State only what the reviewed evidence supports. Identify unresolved questions.

## Verified facts
For each fact, include the exact source, date, relevant passage or page, and limitations.

## Primary documents
List original filings, registry records, court documents, official statistics, archival records and direct URLs.

## Entities and relationships
For each connection, state the legal or documented relationship, start/end dates where known, and the source supporting it. Do not infer control from association.

## Conflicting evidence and alternative explanations
Record material disagreement, missing data and other plausible interpretations.

## Analysis
Clearly separate interpretation from the underlying facts.

## Confidence
Use a written explanation of evidence quality and gaps. Do not use an unexplained numerical score.

## Outstanding questions
List what would need to be established before reaching a stronger conclusion.

## Corrections and review history
Record reviewer, date, change and reason.
