# SECTORX83 Research Standards

## Purpose and scope

SECTORX83 is an independent research project examining institutional power, financial systems, corporate ownership, political influence, surveillance, information governance and public accountability. It is not a government agency, intelligence service, law-enforcement body or holder of classified access. No physical headquarters or institutional affiliation is claimed.

Research is conducted using lawful, publicly accessible or properly authorised sources. The workspace is a research and documentation tool, not a publication of allegations.

## Source hierarchy

1. Original public records: legislation, court judgments, regulatory filings, company registries, public procurement records, audited statements, official statistics and archival documents.
2. First-party institutional publications: annual reports, governance disclosures, grant databases, policy statements and institutional histories. These must be labelled as statements by the publishing institution.
3. Independent reporting and specialist research: used for context, leads and corroboration.
4. Commentary and social media: treated as leads only, never as sole support for a material factual claim.

Where possible, a claim should be checked against the original document rather than a search result or summary. Corporate registry information can be self-reported or incomplete; beneficial ownership claims require corroboration.

## Claim handling

Every material claim must record:
- The exact proposition being made.
- Publisher, document title, URL, publication date and access date.
- The relevant passage, page, filing identifier or record number.
- Whether the source is primary, institutional self-description or secondary reporting.
- Known limitations, conflicts and contrary evidence.
- Review date and researcher notes.

Distinguish verified fact, attributed claim, analysis, hypothesis and unknown. A relationship is not proof of coordination. A donation is not proof of a favour. A family relationship is not proof of shared control. A meeting is not proof of agreement. A company’s size is not proof of unlawful conduct.

## Family and institution research

Research into families such as the Rothschilds and Rockefellers must identify specific people, legal entities, dates and records. Do not imply that a family is a single organisation or that all relatives share the same interests. Separate foundations, companies, trusts, family members and independent boards. Document the source and legal nature of every connection.

## Privacy and safety

Do not collect or publish private addresses, credentials, sensitive personal information, private communications or data obtained through unauthorised access. Do not facilitate harassment, targeting, threats, intrusion or surveillance of private individuals. Public-interest research must be proportionate and lawful.

## Corrections and review

Material errors should be corrected promptly with a dated note. Preserve source links and research history where possible. Unresolved questions remain unresolved; do not fill gaps with speculation.

## Current workspace limitation

The site currently provides curated research frameworks and links to external source registries. It does not yet provide automated ingestion, a live intelligence feed, a populated entity graph, multi-user accounts, role-based permissions or a full audit-log system. Do not describe those capabilities as operational until they are implemented and tested.
